Dependencies

Mandatory (hard logic)

The depot must be built (7.2) before the stockpile can move in (7.3). Integrated testing (9.1) cannot start until the systems exist.

Discretionary

I chose to finish privacy impact assessments (2.2) before any data work on the digital twin begins, even though design could technically overlap.

External

School HVAC work can only happen in July and August, when students are away. So SHIELD work is split into two summer waves (6.2a and 6.2b).

The longest chain of dependent work is: charter, then requirements, then detailed design, then baseline approval, then procurement, then depot land, depot construction, stockpile, integrated testing, Operation Sentinel, go-live and closing. Any delay on this chain delays the whole project. The PMO tracks it weekly, and the depot contract has late-delivery penalties. Click "Critical path" above to see it.

SMART milestones

Milestones on a Gantt chart should be specific, measurable, assignable, realistic and time-framed (Renso, 2026):

  1. M1 Charter approvedFeb 26, 2027 · Owner: Champion

    Measured by: four signatures on the charter

  2. M2 Baseline plan approved (kill point)Sep 3, 2027 · Owner: PM

    Measured by: steering committee vote: continue, redirect or stop

  3. M3 Major contracts awardedNov 30, 2027 · Owner: Procurement lead

    Measured by: signed contracts for depot, lab, HVAC, compute

  4. M4 Go-liveOct 1, 2029 · Owner: Sponsor

    Measured by: Operation Sentinel passed; alert issued within 72 hours

  5. M5 Project closedDec 31, 2029 · Owner: PM

    Measured by: all deliverables accepted, contracts closed, lessons learned filed

Estimating under uncertainty

PERT estimate and project buffer

Estimates are uncertain, so a project buffer should always exist (Renso, 2026). For the riskiest task, depot construction, I used PERT: optimistic 10 months, most likely 13 months, pessimistic 19 months. The PERT estimate is (10 + 4×13 + 19) ÷ 6 = 13.5 months, which is the duration planned on the chart. Go-live is three months before the hard close date, and that gap is the project buffer.

5 days

Controlling the schedule. If a team misses a deadline, I act within five working days (Renso, 2026). A slipped milestone triggers a recovery plan: re-sequencing, adding temporary staff, or using contingency funds. If two milestones slip in a row, the issue goes to the steering committee.
