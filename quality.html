Topic15

Project quality management

Quality management has three processes (Renso, 2026). In Sentinel Valley:

Process 1

Planning quality

Each deliverable gets measurable standards. A school room passes at five or more equivalent air changes per hour (CDC, n.d.). A wastewater result reaches officials within 48 hours. The digital twin's forecasts are checked against real flu-season data.

Process 2

Quality assurance

Independent audits check that we follow our own processes. One example is checking that every data request had a privacy approval.

Process 3

Quality control

We inspect actual results. HVAC flow tests, lab proficiency tests and app penetration tests produce validated deliverables.

I benchmark against existing systems, such as BCCDC wastewater surveillance (BCCDC, n.d.), and use Six Sigma's DMAIC cycle (define, measure, analyze, improve, control) to cut sample turnaround time. For security, the data platform will earn ISO/IEC 27001 certification. ISO certification gives partners outside proof that our processes meet an international standard, which helps when we ask Fraser Health to trust a university with health data.

Topic17

Testing and cost of quality

Testing finds problems, and quality management also prevents them (American Society for Quality [ASQ], n.d.). Our testing plan has four layers:

4 · Operation Sentinel (Sept 2029)A full-scale Disease X exercise with all partners. It must pass before go-live.
3 · User acceptance testingFraser Health and school districts test with their real workflows.
2 · Integration testing (Jun–Aug 2029)Signals flow from site to lab to digital twin to dashboard to the official.
1 · Unit testingEach sensor, lab method and software module on its own.

Testing builds from the bottom up: each layer must pass before the one above it.

The cost of quality is the price of conformance plus the cost of nonconformance (Renso, 2026). The cards show which budget lines cover each category.
