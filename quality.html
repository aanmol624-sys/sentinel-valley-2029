Quality

Course topics on this page: 15. Project quality management 17. Testing & cost of quality

Quality means the project satisfies the needs it was started for (Renso, 2026). For a warning system, a false alarm or a missed signal would both damage trust.

15Project quality management

Quality management has three processes (Renso, 2026). In Sentinel Valley:

  • Planning quality: each deliverable gets measurable standards. A school room passes at five or more equivalent air changes per hour (CDC, n.d.). A wastewater result reaches officials within 48 hours. The digital twin's forecasts are checked against real flu-season data.
  • Quality assurance: independent audits check that we follow our own processes. One example is checking that every data request had a privacy approval.
  • Quality control: we inspect actual results. HVAC flow tests, lab proficiency tests and app penetration tests produce validated deliverables.

I benchmark against existing systems, such as BCCDC wastewater surveillance (BCCDC, n.d.), and use Six Sigma's DMAIC cycle (define, measure, analyze, improve, control) to cut sample turnaround time. For security, the data platform will earn ISO/IEC 27001 certification. ISO certification gives partners outside proof that our processes meet an international standard, which helps when we ask Fraser Health to trust a university with health data.

Cause-and-effect diagram: "an alert reached officials late"

Possible causes, by category: People (sampler on leave, analyst not trained); Process (no weekend lab shift, unclear escalation); Technology (sequencer down, API to Fraser Health failed); Environment (a flood closed roads to the lab). Each cause has a prevention measure, such as backup samplers, a weekend rota, a second sequencer and a road-independent courier plan.

17Testing and cost of quality

Testing finds problems, and quality management also prevents them (American Society for Quality [ASQ], n.d.). Our testing plan has four layers:

  1. Unit testing: each sensor, lab method and software module on its own.
  2. Integration testing (Jun–Aug 2029): signals flow from site to lab to digital twin to dashboard to the official.
  3. User acceptance testing: Fraser Health and school districts test with their real workflows.
  4. Operation Sentinel (Sept 2029): a full-scale Disease X exercise with all partners. It must pass before go-live.

The cost of quality is the price of conformance plus the cost of nonconformance (Renso, 2026). The table shows which budget lines cover each category.
