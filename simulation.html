Day 0: the same outbreak, two endings

This is a fictional scenario that shows how the five pillars work together. A new avian flu variant spills over to a farm worker near Chilliwack in October 2030. Drag the slider to move through the days.

Without Sentinel Valley (like 2020)

  1. Day 0: A poultry worker near Chilliwack develops a fever. Nobody links it to sick birds.
  2. Day 3: The virus spreads in a crowded bunkhouse. No one is tested yet.
  3. Day 9: A cluster of severe pneumonia appears at Chilliwack General Hospital.
  4. Day 14: Samples are sent away for identification; results take days.
  5. Day 21: Cases appear in schools and care homes in Abbotsford and Mission.
  6. Day 30: PPE runs short; supplies must come from outside the valley.
  7. Day 40: Schools and businesses close, and the region goes into lockdown.

With Sentinel Valley (2030)

  1. Day 0: Barn-air sensors at a Chilliwack farm flag an unusual influenza signal. The farm's biosecurity upgrades reduce worker exposure.
  2. Day 1: The UFV genomics lab sequences it within 24 hours: a new H5 variant. CFIA and Fraser Health are notified.
  3. Day 2: The worker's clinic is a sentinel site; he is tested and isolated with support. Wastewater at the JAMES plant is checked.
  4. Day 3: The digital twin runs 5 scenarios overnight; officials choose targeted testing at 12 farms and 2 bunkhouses.
  5. Day 5: Mobile clinics test 1,400 workers; PPE ships from the local depot in hours. The alert app informs residents in English, Punjabi and Spanish.
  6. Day 9: Wastewater shows no community spread. Clean-air schools and care homes stay open.
  7. Day 40: The outbreak is contained to 1 farm cluster. There is no regional shutdown.

The flow behind the scenario

SENSE detects a signal and the lab confirms it within 24 hours, then SIMULATE tests response options in the digital twin. After public-health officials decide, SUPPLY and SHIELD provide testing, PPE and clean air, while SUSTAIN keeps people trained and informed. If no community spread shows up, normal life continues, and every exercise and real event updates the model.

Each step in the right-hand column uses a budget line that exists on the Cost page.
