Topic13

SWOT analysis

Internal strengths and weaknesses, external opportunities and threats.

HelpfulHarmful Internal

Strengths (internal)

  • UFV's Agriculture Centre of Excellence, with a barn that has a poultry area, located among the farms
  • UFV computing and health programs to train local talent
  • Builds on systems that already work, such as BCCDC wastewater testing

Weaknesses (internal)

  • UFV has never run a $1B program
  • No legal authority over public-health orders
  • The digital twin is new technology with uncertainty
External

Opportunities (external)

  • WHO and CEPI urging preparedness for whole pathogen families (WHO, 2024a)
  • Clean air also cuts ordinary flu and cold sick days every year
  • The model could be exported to other farming regions in Canada

Threats (external)

  • A pandemic arriving before 2029
  • Another atmospheric-river flood during construction
  • Public distrust of surveillance; tariffs raising equipment prices

SWOT is useful only if it changes the plan (Renso, 2026). So each quadrant feeds a decision. The strengths (UFV's farm-based research centre and computing programs) are used to seize the biggest opportunity, which is getting ready for whole families of pathogens. The weaknesses become entries in the risk register below: inexperience with a $1B program is handled by a PMP-certified deputy and independent audits, and the lack of legal authority is handled by partner agreements. The threats, such as a flood or an early pandemic, are why the depot sits on high ground and why the surge unit and stockpile arrive before go-live.

Positive risk

Exploit and enhance

Some risks are positive. One is federal co-funding to copy the model in other provinces. I would exploit it by documenting everything as a reusable playbook, and enhance it by inviting PHAC to the Operation Sentinel exercise (Renso, 2026).

Every month

Controlling risk

Controlling risk means re-scoring the register every month, issuing change requests when a response changes the plan, and treating the contingency as a managed reserve.

Topic23

Procurement management

UFV cannot build most of this itself.

  1. Planwhat to buy and when
  2. Conductprocurements and award contracts
  3. Controlvendor relationships
  4. Closecontracts

Procurement has four stages: plan what to buy and when, conduct procurements and award contracts, control vendor relationships, and close contracts (Renso, 2026). Where the solution is open, I use an RFP to ask vendors to propose how. Where the item is clearly specified, I use an RFQ to ask only for price.

RFP

The solution is open: vendors propose how.

RFQ

The item is clearly specified: vendors give only a price.
